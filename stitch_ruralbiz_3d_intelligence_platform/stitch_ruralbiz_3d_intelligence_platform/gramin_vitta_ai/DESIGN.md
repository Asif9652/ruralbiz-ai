## Brand & Style

The design system bridges grassroots rural enterprise with high-velocity sovereign fintech. Engineered for micro-entrepreneurs across semi-urban and rural India, the aesthetic synthesizes deep agrarian vitality with institutional trust and AI intelligence. It eschews generic bureaucratic austerity in favor of a cinematic, tactile, and deeply grounded atmosphere: modern agro-industrial intelligence meets dignified financial empowerment.

### Emotional Demarcation
- **Institutional Gravitas & Dignity:** Assures users that governmental and institutional backing is robust, secure, and permanent.
- **Empowering Intelligence:** Generative advisory outputs feel transparent, predictive, and lucid rather than inscrutable or alienating.
- **Rooted Modernity:** Visual motifs honor the terracotta soils, deep forest canopies, and agrarian harvest tones of rural India, uplifted by electric data filaments.

### Design Movement: Tactical Biophilic Glassmorphism
The aesthetic combines frosted earthen surfaces, tactile micro-affordances, and luminous precision badges. Surface planes mimic polished mica and dew-dusted leaves—layered translucency hovering over dark, nutrient-rich soil backdrops. Tactile action points provide immediate physical feedback, catering to low-literacy clarity and bilingual legibility without compromising cutting-edge fintech poise.

## Colors

The palette draws directly from the rural biome bathed in algorithmic twilight. A foundation of dense, oxygenated forest blacks and wet loam anchors the canvas, while luminous emerald and sovereign terracotta establish semantic priority and institutional warmth.

### Chromatic Hierarchy
- **Primary (`#10B981` — Vibrant Emerald):** Used for primary conversion mechanisms, validated fiscal streams, credit eligibility markers, and affirmative advisory paths.
- **Secondary (`#D97706` — Earth Terracotta / Mustard Grain):** Embodies agricultural capital, subsidy thresholds, crop insurance advisories, and sovereign scheme tags.
- **Tertiary (`#14B8A6` — Restrained Electric Teal):** Reserved specifically for machine-learning telemetry, automated cash-flow reconciliation, and voice-assisted query pulses.
- **Neutral Surface Baseline (`#061A14` — Deep Forest Dark):** A deep, saturated evergreen-slate that replaces pure digital blacks, reducing ocular fatigue under harsh sunlight or low-light village conditions.

### Supporting Semantic & Verification Accents
- **Warm Ivory (`#F7F5EE`):** Primary reading color across display headlines and critical telemetry; softer on high-contrast displays than stark zinc white.
- **Muted Sovereign Gold (`#EAB308`):** Direct visual shorthand for Pradhan Mantri schemes, priority lending reserves, and audit guarantees.
- **Integrity Status Badges:**
  - *Verified Tier:* Luminous Emerald glow (`#10B981` at 15% opacity with `#34D399` solid border).
  - *Estimated Tier:* Muted Amber glow (`#F59E0B` at 15% opacity with `#FBBF24` solid border).
  - *Sample/Hypothetical Data:* Cool Atmospheric Slate (`#64748B` at 18% opacity with `#94A3B8` border).

## Typography

Typography prioritizes high optical clarity, high distinction between structural digits and narrative advice, and clear cross-language balance across Indic glyphs (Devanagari, Tamil) and English.

### Type Pairings
- **Headlines (`Epilogue`):** Editorial, weighted, and grounded with human warmth. Its geometric balance maintains integrity across complex multilingual translations without feeling sterile.
- **Body & Dialogue (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Exceptional x-height, clear counterforms, and open apertures that enhance legibility across budget smartphone displays and varying outdoor ambient conditions.
- **Financial & AI Telemetry (`JetBrains Mono`):** Dedicated to ledger numbers, interest rates, Aadhaar/UDYAM masking tokens, confidence scores, and scheme identifiers, preventing optical misreadings of critical financial values.

### Multilingual & Transliteration Behavior
When dynamically rendering regional languages (e.g., Hindi via Noto Sans Devanagari, Tamil via Noto Sans Tamil), vertical line-height rules automatically expand by a factor of 1.25x across all body and headline levels to prevent clipping of upper and lower diacritical vowel marks (*matras* and *pullis*).

## Layout & Spacing

The structural layout utilizes a fluid 12-column adaptive grid that shifts smoothly across three core form factors:
- **Mobile (360px - 599px):** 4-column framework, `1rem` outer canvas margin, `1.25rem` column gutters. All advisory cards expand to full available track width (`col-span-4`).
- **Tablet / Micro-Kiosk (600px - 1023px):** 8-column system, `2rem` margins, enabling concurrent side-by-side assessment of ledger sheets and AI chat diagnostics.
- **Desktop / Institutional Admin (1024px+):** 12-column system, max container width of 1440px with `4rem` outer gutters, creating deep editorial breathing room.

### Spacing Philosophy
Rhythm follows a disciplined 8-point base grid (expressed as `0.5rem` / `8px` steps). Complex financial datasets and agricultural ledger rows utilize compact vertical density (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) to maximize spatial efficiency, whereas AI advisory cards and interactive voice touchpoints use expansive spacing (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to prevent accidental taps on capacitive touchscreen hardware.

## Elevation & Depth

Spatial hierarchy is rendered through biophilic glassmorphism layered over deep ambient loam backdrops. Rather than flat neutral shadows, surfaces feature low-opacity emerald and moss ambient halos that create depth without visual noise.

### Layer Architecture
- **Base Canvas (Level 0):** Deepest background (`#04120D`), matte, absorbing light.
- **Elevated Surfaces (Level 1 — Ledger & Analytics Deck):** Translucent composite (`#0B251C` at 70% opacity) treated with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and bounded by a sub-pixel highlight border (`#10B981` at 12% opacity).
- **Interactive Modals & Advisory Nodes (Level 2):** Elevated floating glass (`#0E3326` at 85% opacity, `backdrop-filter: blur(24px)`), framed with a crisp inner edge stroke and an ambient drop shadow: `0 20px 40px -15px rgba(2, 14, 10, 0.75), 0 0 20px 2px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.08)`.
- **Telemetry Indicators & Floating Badges (Level 3):** Solid jewel-coat badges elevated with high-frequency neon back-glows to indicate system processing states or active voice capture.

## Shapes

The design uses balanced, natural contours (`roundedness: 2`). Standard structural surfaces, cards, and input fields apply an 8px (`0.5rem`) radius; elevated modular cards and dashboard containers use 16px (`1rem`); larger floating advisory sheets employ 24px (`1.5rem`). 

Pill forms (`rounded-full`) are strictly preserved for status badges, numeric telemetry chips, audio recording triggers, and bilingual language toggles. This establishes clear functional differentiation between actionable status indicators (fully rounded) and structural data containers (subtly curved).

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Affordances
- **Primary CTA (Financial Execution / Scheme Application):** Constructed with a vibrant emerald gradient base (`#10B981` to `#059669`), paired with `#031710` bold text. Includes a physical, tactile click offset (`translate-y-0.5` active state) and a subtle 1px inner ambient rim on the top edge (`rgba(255,255,255,0.25)`).
- **Secondary (Government Scheme Reference / Download):** Glassmorphic surface (`#0D2B20` at 60% opacity) bounded by an earthy terracotta border (`#D97706` at 40% opacity), shifting to full opacity on focus or tap.
- **Voice-Assistant Query Trigger:** Tactile floating circular pill button flanked by pulsing rings rendered with electric teal (`#14B8A6`), accompanied by real-time acoustic waveform feedback.

### Data Integrity Badges
- **Verified Badge:** Compact pill container housing an emerald checkmark glyph, `#10B981` text, and a glowing green backdrop halo (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`).
- **Estimated Badge:** Terracotta amber pill with a dotted radar icon, indicating algorithmic approximation from regional micro-market surveys.
- **Sample/Model Badge:** Translucent neutral gray pill with a segmented border, denoting simulated repayment or cash-flow scenarios.

### Cards & Ledger Blocks
- Glassmorphic panels with top-edge ambient illumination.
- Segmented header separating business intelligence summaries from tabular cash-flow breakdowns.
- Integrated quick-action footer containing localized audio playback toggles ("Listen to this summary in Hindi / Tamil").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Dark translucent fill (`rgba(6, 26, 20, 0.8)`) with a sturdy 1.5px baseline border tinted in deep forest sage (`#1B4336`).
- Direct-focus shift illuminates the bounding border with electric teal (`#14B8A6`) alongside an ambient outer aura (`0 0 0 3px rgba(20, 184, 166, 0.2)`).
- Form inputs support immediate speech-to-text mic triggers pinned to the interior right margin.

### Bilingual Toggle Bar
- Floating segmented pill switch positioned at the utility header.
- Selected state activates a warm ivory glass card with solid emerald labeling, ensuring clear contextual awareness of the active language profile across multi-user family kiosks.
