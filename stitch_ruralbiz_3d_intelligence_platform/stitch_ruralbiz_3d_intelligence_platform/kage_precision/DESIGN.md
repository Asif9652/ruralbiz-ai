## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes the quiet discipline of Japanese *shou sugi ban* (charred cedar wood, deep sumi ink, layered mist) with the analytical severity of sovereign fintech intelligence. It balances monolithic stillness with acute precision.

### Target Audience & Emotional Intent
- **Audience:** Institutional operators, quantitative strategists, sovereign asset allocators, and technical executives demanding low visual noise, absolute clarity, and deliberate tactile restraint.
- **Emotional Response:** Induces intense focus, unyielding security, deliberate contemplation, and authority. Elements feel carved, measured, and permanent.

### Visual Architecture & Aesthetic Movements
- **Sumi & Charred Timber Depth:** Monochromatic surfaces constructed of dense, carbonized blacks (`#0a0a0c`, `#111114`, `#1a1a1f`) that simulate the layered grain of scorched timber and ink washes.
- **Atmospheric Mist & Incense:** Ghosted gradients, linear edge-lights, and localized vermilion glows cutting through deep space rather than traditional drop shadows.
- **Sovereign Editorial Precision:** Hyper-refined typographic hierarchies using high-contrast scales, razor-thin hairlines (`rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)` to `0.12`), and compact data nodes mimicking ledger seals and technical instrument dials.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in elemental carbon and warm fibrous paper, punctuated by singular strokes of ceremonial vermilion.

### Primary Spectrum
- **Primary Vermilion (`#e0231c`):** The catalytic accent. Reserved for critical data thresholds, primary calls to execution, active state pips, and deliberate telemetry accents.
- **Tertiary Crimson Glow (`#ff4d46`):** Used strictly for optical halos, active state transitions, and glowing micro-indicators against charred surfaces.

### Neutral & Tonal Canvas
- **Paper Light (`#f5f2eb`):** Primary display headers, key numeric values, and stark contrast elements. Evokes raw mulberry washi paper.
- **Paper Muted (`#8e8d88`):** Secondary metadata, sub-labels, and structural column definitions.
- **Paper Ghost (`#454440`):** Inactive icons, subtle grid ticks, and secondary borders.
- **Void Obsidian (`#0a0a0c`):** Base canvas layer. Deepest charred tier.
- **Charred Surface Layer 1 (`#111114`):** Base card backgrounds and persistent container surfaces.
- **Charred Surface Layer 2 (`#1a1a1f`):** Interactive containers, hover surfaces, elevated panel modules.
- **Charred Surface Layer 3 (`#222228`):** Floating controls, tooltips, active popovers, and drawer surfaces.

### Semantic Tones
- **Success (Pine Smolder):** `#2e7d5a`
- **Warning (Raw Amber):** `#d97706`
- **Critical / Risk:** Coincides with Primary Vermilion (`#e0231c`)

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy enforces high-tension discipline between sculptural geometric headers, clear running prose, and ultra-dense monospaced telemetry.

### Role Assignments
- **Headlines (Sora):** Carries structural gravitas and deliberate architectural geometry. Used exclusively for section titles, modal headers, major KPI displays, and high-level index titles. Never set lighter than medium weight.
- **Body (Hanken Grotesk):** Neutral, hyper-legible humanist-grotesque balancing modern European geometry with Japanese editorial restraint. Renders institutional reports, narrative telemetry, and interface interaction descriptions.
- **Telemetry & Labels (JetBrains Mono):** The sovereign financial instrument layer. Renders currency values, algorithmic timestamps, balance sheets, table headers, and pill tags. Set in tabular figures (`tnum`) by default.

## Layout & Spacing

Layout conforms to an asymmetric, architectural grid inspired by Japanese *ma* (deliberate negative space) coupled with high-density financial matrices.

### Grid Rhythm & Adapting Dimensions
- **Desktop (1440px+):** 12-column dynamic framework with `2rem` gutters and `3rem` boundary margins. Data terminals and monitoring views can unlock full width via a rigid 16-column matrix with `1rem` gutters.
- **Tablet (768px - 1439px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` gutters and `2rem` margins. Complex financial tables stack secondary metadata under primary assets rather than scrolling horizontally.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column column system with `1rem` gutters and `1rem` outer margins. Fixed floating navigation docked to screen bottom with compact vertical paddings.

### Component Spacing Principles
- Element gaps leverage the strict linear rhythm (`space-xs` through `space-xl`).
- High internal padding is favored inside cards (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) to establish calm, monastic focus surrounding high-density numerical information.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth rejects standard muddy drop shadows, replacing them with sharp, low-contrast ink outlines and subtle vermilion back-glows.

### The Charred Tonal Stack
Depth is achieved primarily through value-shifted timber tiers:
1. **Canvas (Depth 0):** `#0a0a0c` — Infinite background substrate.
2. **Foundational Card (Depth 1):** `#111114` — Bounded by `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`.
3. **Interactive Surface (Depth 2):** `#1a1a1f` — Hover layers, selected rows, active tools. Bounded by `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.12)`.
4. **Floating Ledger (Depth 3):** `#222228` — Command palettes, flyouts, and contextual detail sheets.

### Lighting & Atmospheric Effects
- **Hairline Boundaries:** Surface division relies on precise, semi-translucent borders: `border: 1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`. Never use opaque gray borders.
- **Vermilion Incandescence:** Active focus rings, critical indicators, and premier interaction surfaces leverage radiant glowing penumbras:
  - `box-shadow: 0 0 0 1px #e0231c, 0 0 24px -4px rgba(224, 35, 28, 0.35)`
- **Atmospheric Mist Layer:** Backdrops beneath modals and popovers deploy dense hardware-accelerated frosted layers: `backdrop-filter: blur(16px) saturate(140%)` coupled with `background: rgba(10, 10, 12, 0.82)`.

## Shapes

The shape architecture relies on taut, near-sharp geometries evoking precision-machined slate, chiseled joinery, and cut washi card stock.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Panels, Windows, and Cards:** `0.25rem` (4px). Clean, razor-like perimeter that aligns strictly to the grid without visual softness.
- **Buttons & Input Fields:** `0.25rem` (4px). Structural and tactile.
- **Data Pills & Status Nodes:** Full circle/pill (`9999px`) to create intentional geometric contrast against the rigid rectilinear modules.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Execution Button:** Background `#e0231c`, text `#f5f2eb`, font `JetBrains Mono` 11px uppercase tracking `0.06em`, border-radius `0.25rem`. On hover, background shifts to `#ff4d46` with a localized vermilion halo (`0 0 20px rgba(224, 35, 28, 0.4)`).
- **Secondary Ghost Button:** Background `transparent`, text `#f5f2eb`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.12)`. On hover, background shifts to `#1a1a1f` with border elevated to `rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.25)`.
- **Destructive / Alert Action:** Background `#111114`, border `1px solid #e0231c`, text `#e0231c`. Hover triggers solid `#e0231c` fill with `#f5f2eb` text.

### Data Pill Tags & Indicators
- Compact geometric identifiers set in `JetBrains Mono` 10px uppercase.
- Neutral pill: Background `#1a1a1f`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)`, text `#8e8d88`.
- Active Telemetry Pill: Background `rgba(224, 35, 28, 0.12)`, border `1px solid rgba(224, 35, 28, 0.4)`, text `#f5f2eb`, featuring a leading 4px glowing dot (`#e0231c`).

### Cards & Container Modules
- Base structure: `#111114` canvas, `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.08)` border, padded with `1.5rem`.
- Section division: Instead of horizontal lines across the entire card, use inset hairline borders or pure `1rem` negative space paired with micro-labels.

### Lists & Ledger Rows
- Height `44px` for high-density, `56px` for standard accounts.
- Separated by `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.04)`.
- Row hover states transition to `#16161b` with a 2px left border accent in `#e0231c`.
- Numerics render right-aligned in `JetBrains Mono` with explicit currency anchors.

### Form Inputs
- Background: `#0a0a0c`. Border: `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.12)`. Text: `#f5f2eb`. Placeholder: `#454440`.
- Padding: `0.625rem 0.875rem`. Font: `Hanken Grotesk` 14px for natural inputs, `JetBrains Mono` 13px for quantitative values.
- Active state: Border transitions to `#e0231c`, accompanied by a subtle 1px outline glow (`rgba(224, 35, 28, 0.25)`).

### Checkboxes & Segmented Toggles
- Checkbox: `16px x 16px` square with `2px` corners. Base: `#111114`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.2)`. Selected state: `#e0231c` fill with a paper-white checkmark.
- Segmented Control: Housed inside an `#0a0a0c` carved trough. Active segment pops with `#1a1a1f` background, crisp `rgba(245, 242, 235, 0.1)` hairline outline, and white text.

### Specialized Component: Algorithmic Seal
- Circular sovereign emblem (32px) stamped with a central Japanese kanji or geometric monogram, functioning as an authentication badge or verification check on financial settlement events.
